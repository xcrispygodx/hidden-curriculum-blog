◆ The Containment Myth

All official communications since 2011 have contained a central claim: Fukushima is "under control." Prime Minister Yoshihide Suga announced in 2020 that "treated" radioactive water would be "safe to drink." The International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) released a report in July 2023 endorsing Japan's plan to discharge over 1.3 million metric tons of radioactive water into the Pacific Ocean.

Each of these claims collapses upon examination.
