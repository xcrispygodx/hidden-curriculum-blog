Induce localized precipitation: Ionospheric heating alters atmospheric pressure gradients, creating artificial high and low pressure systems. Dr. Eastlund's original patent (US Patent 4,686,605) described "a method and apparatus for altering a region in the earth's atmosphere, ionosphere, and/or magnetosphere."

Disable satellite electronics: The same beam can create artificial plasma clouds that absorb, refract, or scatter satellite communications across a 1,000-mile diameter area.

Generate over-the-horizon radar: The ionospheric mirror created by HAARP reflections can bounce radar signals to targets 6,000 miles away—beyond line of sight.
